Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours in a single playthrough. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10; the hardest trophy is A Long Road (100% Completion). Sources: GameMapa, Push Square.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5 60–80 hours

    Play the story and pick up side missions as they appear. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app as you go. Do the first meditation in Chapter 1 — it is the one missable trophy (see Positive Mental Attitude below). Story trophies and the stranger questlines unlock along the way.

  2. Stage 2 — The 100% Completion checklist about 100 hours

    After the final mission the checklist appears under Progress. It asks for almost everything — 15 of the 18 card sets, 70 of the 80 Challenges, every robbery, every shooting range and drone race — but not literally everything. Work through it with the filters below.

  3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup about 10 hours

    The checklist does not ask for the Strangers & Freaks strands, so they are usually what is left, together with stray trophies and the five hidden trophies nobody has solved yet. Finish the 8 strands, then the platinum pops.
